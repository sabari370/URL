Protection Against "Quishing" Attacks

QR Phishing (Quishing) tricks victims into scanning physical or email QR barcodes on mobile devices, where URL inspection is more difficult. PhishGuard AI decodes the payload, presents the decoded address for human inspection, and evaluates structural risks before you ever open it.
